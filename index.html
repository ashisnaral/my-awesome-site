About Me

Welcome, traveler of the information superhighway! I'm AK Naral — songwriter, author, entrepreneur, and proud Non-Resident Nepali (NRN).

I wrote my songs in my early twenties — a time when I was broke, broken, and often hungover. I once told myself: "I'm going to copyright this sentiment someday." Today, I own the copyright to every single one of them.

Rather than inflict my own voice on the world, I hired talented session artists to re-record my compositions. The music stayed. The cringe went away.

Want to cover my songs? Go for it. Want to collaborate? Hit me up on Instagram at @ak_naral.
